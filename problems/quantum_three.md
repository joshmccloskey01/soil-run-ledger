# Three open problems — physics

Loaded 2026-10-28. Josh's wording, verbatim. Checks and notes are in NOTES.md (2026-10-28), not here.

1. **Standard quantum mechanics has two rules that clash.** Waves evolve smoothly, except during a measurement, when they jump. But a measuring device is made of atoms, so it should follow the smooth rule too. The theory never says where the line is.
2. **Many Worlds has no meaning for odds.** If every outcome happens, "50% chance" has nothing to be about. The odds rule has to be added from outside, and the attempts to derive it are still fought over.
3. **Relativity and quantum disagree about a mass in two places.** Quantum allows one ball to sit in two spots at once. Relativity needs the ball in one spot to say how space curves. Penrose's point is that it can't do both.
